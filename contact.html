Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and process

How quickly can you start my project?

Most projects can begin within 1-2 weeks after contract signing. Rush projects may be accommodated based on our current workload.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment plans for projects over KES 100,000. Typically 50% upfront, 25% at midpoint, and 25% on completion.

What's included in the free consultation?

A 30-60 minute discussion about your project goals, timeline, budget, and our recommended approach. No obligations, just valuable insights.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Absolutely! While we're based in Kenya, we serve clients across East Africa and globally through remote collaboration.
